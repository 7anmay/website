Previously

I also did some work on agentic robotics before it was cool with professor Jean Oh at the BIG Lab. I co-founded Fambo, an agritech startup, where I led AI and robotics. Before that I was at Addverb, where I led the first ever deployment of a fleet of AMRs in the company's and the country's history. And it all started at ZINE at MNIT Jaipur with professor Rajesh Kumar.

ORB — healthcare robots for the operating room, developed with UPMC.
